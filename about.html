About Intemo

We're building the AI workforce for freight.

Freight runs on people doing repetitive, high-stakes work under constant time pressure. Intemo gives those teams a tireless set of AI agents, so the operational grind stops being the bottleneck to growth.

24/7always on
Our mission

Take the busywork out of moving the world's cargo.

Every shipment passes through a chain of manual steps, reading email, chasing rates, drafting quotes, keying data into systems. We believe that work should run itself, reliably and transparently, so people can focus on customers and decisions.

Intemo agents don't replace your team. They remove the grind that keeps your team from doing their best work.

manual mess → automated flow
How we build

Principles behind every agent.

Operate, don't observe

Our agents take real actions inside your tools, not dashboards that tell you what you already know.

Trust by transparency

Every decision is logged and reviewable. You can see what an agent did and why, on every task.

Fit the existing stack

We work inside your inbox and ERP. No rip-and-replace, no new system to learn.
