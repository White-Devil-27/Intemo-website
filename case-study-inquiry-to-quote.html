Case study · Inquiry to Quote Automation

From a buried inbox to quotes in minutes.

How a leading enterprise freight forwarder automated its entire inquiry-to-quote desk with Intemo, and saw the revenue impact inside the first quarter.

Enterprise freight forwarder 12 branches, pan-India 1000+ inquiries / month Air & sea
The client

A leading enterprise freight forwarder in India, handling 1000+ inquiries every month across air and sea, with 12 branches operating pan-India.

The pain point

A quoting desk drowning in manual work.

Manual email reading, logging and data entry for every single inquiry.

Rate fetching across multiple carrier portals was slow and error-prone.

Quotes built from scratch in Excel each time, no templates, no automation.

No centralised tracking, 15–20% of inquiries missed at peak.

Our solution

One automated flow, inbox to client.

Intemo's agents took over the full inquiry-to-quote loop, reading the inbox, fetching rates, building the quote and sending it back.

01

Inquiry received

Only RFQ & inquiry emails were read automatically from the inbox.

02

Rates fetched

Pulled instantly from multiple sources, rate sheets, spot rates, agents & carrier emails.

03

Quotation built

A professional template auto-populated from the fetched rates, with all fields filled.

04

Sent to client

Quotes dispatched in minutes, not hours or days, with minimal manual effort.
